Accordion

Why four themes?
Because the brand has four moods. Labradorite is the brain when it's running, opalite is the brain at peace, jadeite is the system turned all the way up, and quartz is the on-the-record brain built for evidence, screen readers, e-readers, and print.
Can I add a color?
Only if you can name the mineral. If you can't name it, you don't need it.
Where does the heart go?
In the lockup. Always. If the design can't hold it, redesign the container, not the lockup.
